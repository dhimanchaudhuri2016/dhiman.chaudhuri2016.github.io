Khudiram Bose was eighteen when he was executed by the British colonial government on 11 August 1908. This five-minute documentary follows his path from Bengal's revolutionary movement to the Muzaffarpur attack, trial and enduring place in public memory.

His story includes courage, colonial repression and a tragic consequence: the attack intended for Douglas Kingsford fatally injured the wife and daughter of barrister Pringle Kennedy. Remembering the whole story means remembering them too.
